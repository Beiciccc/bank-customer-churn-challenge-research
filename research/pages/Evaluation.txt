Evaluation  Submissions are evaluated on [area under the ROC curve](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Receiver_operating_characteristic) between the predicted probability and the observed target.


## Submission File
For each `id` in the test set, you must predict the probability for the target variable `Exited`. The file should contain a header and have the following format:

    id,Exited
    165034,0.9
    165035,0.1
    165036,0.5
    etc.
